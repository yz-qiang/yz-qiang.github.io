  <p class="intro-panel__lead">I work at the intersection of Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence.</p>
  <p>As a member of the <a href="http://www.se.fudan.edu.cn/">CodeWisdom Group</a>, I am fortunate to work under the guidance of <a href="https://cspengxin.github.io/">Prof. Xin Peng</a>. My research spans <strong>AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)</strong> and <strong>Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI)</strong>, with a particular interest in large language models, knowledge graphs, code intelligence, and reliable AI-powered software development.</p>
